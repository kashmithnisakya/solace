## Near to the broken-hearted
Psalm 34:18 says: "Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit." Many Christians find comfort that God is close in pain, not distant.

## Rest for the weary
In Matthew 11:28, Jesus says: "Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest." This invitation is open to anyone who is exhausted or overwhelmed.

## Lament is allowed
The Psalms are full of honest cries of sadness, anger and confusion. Bringing real feelings to God in prayer, rather than pretending to be fine, is part of the tradition.

## Faith and seeking help
Seeing a doctor or counsellor is not a lack of faith. Many churches encourage professional care alongside prayer and community, and a pastor or trusted church member can also offer support.
