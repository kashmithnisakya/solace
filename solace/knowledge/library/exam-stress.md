## Some stress is normal
Feeling nervous before exams is normal and can even help focus. It becomes a problem when it stops you sleeping, eating or studying.

## Practical things that help
Break revision into short sessions with regular breaks. Make a realistic plan rather than trying to do everything. Keep sleeping, eating and moving, because they help memory and concentration. Talking to friends, family or a teacher about how you feel can take some of the weight off.

## Perspective
Exams matter, but they don't decide your worth or your whole future. There are usually other paths and second chances.
