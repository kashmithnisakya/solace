## Why people self-harm
Self-harm is usually a way of coping with overwhelming feelings, not attention seeking. People who self-harm deserve care and understanding, not judgement.

## Getting through an urge
Urges usually rise and then fade. Things that can help ride one out: waiting fifteen minutes and then seeing how you feel, going somewhere with other people, calling or messaging someone, slow breathing, holding something cold, going for a walk, or writing down what you feel.

## Getting support
Talking to a doctor, counsellor, or a crisis line can help with the feelings underneath. If someone is badly hurt or in danger, emergency help is needed right away.
