## Anger is a normal feeling
Anger is a normal emotion, often a signal that something feels unfair or that we feel hurt or threatened. It becomes a problem when it is frequent, intense, or leads to hurting ourselves or others.

## In the moment
Notice the early signs, like a tight chest, clenched jaw or racing heart. Count slowly, breathe out slowly, and if you can, step away from the situation until you feel calmer. Physical activity can help release the tension.

## Afterwards
When calm, it can help to think about what triggered the anger and what lay underneath it, like hurt, fear or exhaustion, and to talk it through with someone. If anger is causing problems, a doctor can point to anger management support.
