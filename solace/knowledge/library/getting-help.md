## What talking therapy is
Talking therapies, like counselling and cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), mean talking with a trained professional about thoughts, feelings and problems, and learning ways to cope. They help with stress, anxiety, low mood, grief, relationship problems and much more.

## How to start
A family doctor is often a good first step: they can listen, check for physical causes, and suggest treatment or a referral. In Sri Lanka, the free National Mental Health Helpline (1926) can also talk things through and point to local services.

## It's okay to ask
Many people wait a long time before asking for help, worried it means they are weak or "crazy". It doesn't. Getting help early often makes recovery easier.
