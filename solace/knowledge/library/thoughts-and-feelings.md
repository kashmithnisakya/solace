## Thoughts are not facts
When we are stressed or low, the mind often jumps to the worst conclusion, or treats one bad moment as proof that everything is bad. Common patterns include all-or-nothing thinking, predicting disaster, mind reading ("they think I'm useless"), and being very harsh on ourselves.

## Questioning a thought gently
It can help to write the thought down and ask: what is the evidence for and against it? What would I say to a friend who had this thought? Is there a kinder, more balanced way to see it? The goal is not forced positivity, just a fairer view.
