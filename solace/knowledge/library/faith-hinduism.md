## Doing your part, letting go of results
In the Bhagavad Gita (2.47), Krishna teaches that we have a right to our actions, but not to their fruits. Doing one's best and then releasing the outcome can ease the pressure of exams, work, or family expectations.

## Lifting yourself up
The Gita (6.5) says a person should lift themselves up by their own mind and not degrade themselves, because the mind can be one's friend or one's enemy. Treating one's own mind with patience, as a friend would, is part of this teaching.

## Practices that calm
Prayer, japa (repeating a name of God or a mantra), and gentle breathing practices (pranayama) are part of many Hindu traditions and can bring calm.

## Faith and seeking help
Seeking help from a doctor or counsellor is fully compatible with devotion. Caring for one's health is part of caring for the gift of life.
