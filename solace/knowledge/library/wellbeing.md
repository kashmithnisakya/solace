## Five things that help
Research points to five everyday habits that support wellbeing: connecting with other people, being physically active, learning something new, giving to others (even small kindnesses), and paying attention to the present moment. None needs to be big. A short walk, a call with a friend, or noticing the sky for a minute all count.
