## With hardship comes ease
The Qur'an says twice, in Surah Ash-Sharh (94:5-6), that with hardship comes ease. Many Muslims hold on to this as a promise that difficulty is not the whole story.

## Remembrance and rest
Surah Ar-Ra'd (13:28) says that hearts find rest in the remembrance of Allah. Dhikr, dua (personal prayer in your own words) and salah can bring calm and a sense of being heard.

## Patience is not silence
Sabr (patience) does not mean hiding pain or coping alone. The Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, taught that Allah has not created a disease without also creating its cure (Sahih al-Bukhari 5678). Many scholars teach that seeking treatment, including for the mind, is encouraged. Struggling is not a sign of weak faith.

## Not beyond what you can bear
Surah Al-Baqarah (2:286) says Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear. This is meant as comfort, never as a reason to dismiss how heavy someone's pain feels. Reaching out for help is part of bearing it.
