## What depression is
Depression is a common mental health condition. It is different from ordinary ups and downs. It involves a low mood or loss of interest and pleasure in activities for long periods, along with things like poor concentration, feelings of guilt or low self-worth, hopelessness about the future, disturbed sleep, changes in appetite, and feeling very tired.

## It is treatable
Effective treatments exist, including talking therapies and, in some cases, medicines prescribed by a doctor. Many people recover. Asking a doctor or counsellor for help is a sign of strength, not weakness.

## Things that help alongside treatment
Keeping up activities that used to be enjoyable, staying connected to friends and family, regular exercise even if it is a short walk, regular sleep and eating habits, and avoiding alcohol and drugs, which can make depression worse.

## When it feels very dark
Depression can bring thoughts of death or suicide. These are a sign of how much pain someone is in, and real help exists. Talking to a crisis line or someone trusted right away matters.
