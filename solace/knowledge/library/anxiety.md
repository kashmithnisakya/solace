## Everyday anxiety and anxiety disorders
Feeling anxious sometimes is a normal part of life. With an anxiety disorder, the fear and worry are excessive, hard to control, and get in the way of daily life for a long time. Anxiety disorders are among the most common mental health conditions, and they are treatable.

## What helps
Talking therapies, especially cognitive behavioural therapy, are effective. Some people also benefit from medicine prescribed by a doctor. Day to day, regular physical activity, steady sleep, limiting caffeine and alcohol, relaxation and breathing practice, and staying connected to people can all help.
