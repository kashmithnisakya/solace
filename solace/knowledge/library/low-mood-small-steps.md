## Why doing a little helps
When mood is low, people often stop doing things they used to enjoy or need to do. That makes sense, energy is low, but it can make mood drop further. Gently adding back small activities, even before you feel like it, can slowly lift mood. Motivation often follows action rather than coming first.

## Starting tiny
Pick one small thing: a short walk, a shower, texting a friend, tidying one corner. Make it small enough that it feels doable today. Notice how you feel afterwards, even a little better counts.

## Mix of things
It helps to have a mix of things that feel good (a favourite song, time outside), things that give a sense of achievement (a small task done), and things that connect you with people.
