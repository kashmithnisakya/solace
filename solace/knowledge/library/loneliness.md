## Loneliness is common
Anyone can feel lonely, including people surrounded by others. It is about feeling disconnected, not just about being alone. It is a common human feeling, not a personal failing.

## Small ways to reconnect
Reaching out to one person, even with a short message. Joining a group around something you enjoy, such as a sport, class, faith community or volunteering. Saying hello to neighbours or regulars at a shop. Small, repeated contact builds connection over time.

## Being kind to yourself
Loneliness can make people withdraw and think others don't want them around. Noticing that thought, and taking one small step anyway, can break the cycle.
