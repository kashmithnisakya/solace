## Slow breathing
Sit or lie somewhere comfortable. Breathe in gently through the nose, counting to about four, then breathe out slowly through the mouth, counting to about five or six, without forcing it. Keep going for three to five minutes. A longer out-breath helps the body switch from alarm to calm.

## Grounding with the senses (5, 4, 3, 2, 1)
When anxiety feels overwhelming, it can help to come back to the present through the senses: notice five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste. It gives the anxious mind something steady to hold.

## Practice helps
These exercises work best when practised regularly, not only in the worst moments, so the body learns them.
