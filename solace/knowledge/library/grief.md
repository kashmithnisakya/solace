## There is no right way to grieve
Grief can bring shock, numbness, deep sadness, anger, guilt, tiredness, and trouble sleeping. Feelings often come in waves. There is no fixed timetable and no right way to feel. Loss is not only about death: breakups, losing a job, or a change in health can bring grief too.

## What can help
Talking about the person or what was lost, with friends, family or a counsellor. Keeping simple routines, eating and sleeping as well as possible, and being patient with yourself. Marking anniversaries or remembering in your own way can help.

## When to get more support
If grief feels unbearable, goes on without easing, or you are struggling to cope with daily life, talking to a doctor or a bereavement counsellor can really help.
