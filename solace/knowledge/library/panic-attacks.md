## What a panic attack is
A panic attack is a sudden rush of intense fear with strong physical feelings: a pounding heart, shaking, sweating, feeling faint, tight chest or short breath. It can feel like something terrible is happening, but panic attacks themselves are not dangerous and usually pass within minutes.

## During an attack
Try not to fight it. Stay where you are if it is safe, breathe slowly and deeply, and remind yourself that it will pass. Focusing on something steady, like your feet on the floor or slow counting, can help.

## Getting help
If panic attacks keep happening, a doctor can help. Talking therapies such as CBT work well for panic. If chest pain or breathing problems are new or you are not sure what is happening, it is right to get medical help to check.
